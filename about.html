Our kitchen



Our passionate chefs create dishes full of
international flavor, giving the Bay Area
twist that makes them so unique. At
Gochilas Catering, we pride
ourselves on using fresh, sustainable
ingredients in new and delectable ways.
From Mexicali appetizers to Asian-
inspired desserts, our chefs draw
inspiration from every corner of the
earth and fill your menu with flavor.



And our food does more than just taste
good—we believe it should also be a
striking work of art. At every event, bold
flavors and stunning presentation
combine to create an unforgettable
occasion for each guest. No matter your
guests' dietary needs, we can create
something that they’ll love.




Chef-Made

After helping to open several restaurants
in the Bay Area, Chef Anthony Barr
brought his years of culinary expertise to
Gochilas Catering, where he
works alongside his wife Anna, a former
pastry chef who now manages sales and
marketing. Their passion for perfectly
prepared cuisine with innovative flavors
is at the center of every dish Gochilas
creates.



Sustainable



Locally sourcing our ingredients,
conserving water, checking our serving
materials and more—we consider every
element of catering and make sure that
our process aligns with the sustainability
standards we hold. We value and love
the environment that gives us both
warm summer days and delicious
produce, like perfectly ripened avocados
and sweet berries.



Food for All



We understand that people have
different dietary needs and have
adapted our cuisine to accommodate
any diet. Gluten-free, vegan, paleo—you
name it, we’ll make sure that every guest
has something incredible to eat.





Story

Gochilas Catering began with
Chef Anthony’s love of food, cultivated
on his grandparents’ farm in Georgia. In
the mornings, he would pick fresh
vegetables in the garden before helping
his florist grandmother with floral
arrangements in the barn. At dinner,
though, his love of food truly bloomed as
he cooked in the kitchen with his
family’s recipes.

His passion for excellent cuisine drove
every step of his career, so he knew that
in order to prepare the food he wanted
and work for a business he was proud of,
he would need to create his own catering
company.

When his wife and co-owner Anna
Intertas-Barr joined in 2007, she utilized
her training as a pastry chef and crafted
our desserts. After time, she realized her
work ethic in the office and dedication to
clients were her strengths and took over
the front-end of the business, overseeing
all departments and assisting with sales,
administrative tasks and marketing
efforts. Anna's determination to provide
the bustling San Francisco area with
simple ordering and delicious catering is
crucial to our operations.

Since our inception in 2005, we’ve
worked hard to sustain those values,
keeping our entrepreneurial spirit and
our love of high-quality ingredients and
menus in every part of what we do.
Though we trace our roots to comforting
Southern dishes, we pride ourselves on
drawing inspiration from across the
globe and transforming that on a plate.
