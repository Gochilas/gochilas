The Easy Way to Feed Your
Team



No matter how big or small your order,
we’ll follow these quick steps to plan an
incredible, productivity-boosting meal.



Tell us what
you're thinking



From ideal dates to menu inspiration,
event themes and dietary needs, this
is the step where you share with us all
of your wants, needs and dreams.
Once we share your vision, we can get
to work creating the exact meal you
imagined.



Leave the
details to us



Next, we pin down the event
schedule, including delivery time,
set-up space and more. Your menu
will be finalized, and if you have the
time, you might join us for a
complimentary tasting.



Eat exactly 
when you want



On the day of, our professional drivers
will strictly follow the schedule and
arrive well within time to make sure
your meal is seamless.



And if you want



Relax, we'll set-
up & clean-up


Our expert staff will arrange any
décor items and the catering station.
Everything will be seamlessly set-up &
cleaned-up, so you and your guests
can enjoy a relaxing meal.

The Main Course


After 15 years in the San Francisco
restaurant industry, Chef Anthony Barr
combines his southern roots, California
experience and love of global flavors in
Gochilas cuisine. Fresh,
sustainable ingredients are central to
our food and are featured in every dish
we prepare. We balance international
inspiration and local ingredients
throughout our menus.

At Gochilas, we also believe that
every guest deserves to taste something
amazing. Whether you need to
accommodate gluten-free, vegan, paleo
or any other diet, we’ll make sure that
each person enjoys a flavorful, high-
quality meal.
